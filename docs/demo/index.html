Things to try

  • Click Flat slice in the cream. The triangle lies at z = 4, so the whole image is cream: the axial view of 3D Slicer.
  • Click Upright cut through all layers. The three layers show one above the other: chocolate at the top, cream in the middle, biscuit at the bottom.
  • Click Triangle that leaves the cake and check a black square inside the triangle. Its point is outside the cake, so it gets 0.
  • Swap b and c. The direction to the right turns around, and the image comes out mirrored.
  • Move a slowly to the side. When the legs differ by more than half a voxel a warning appears, but the triangle is still drawn.

How a square gets its value

The image is a sheet of squares, one voxel apart in both directions, laid on the plane of the triangle. Each square has a point in the volume, and takes the value of the nearest voxel. Squares outside the triangle, and points outside the volume, get 0.

point(row, col) = corner + col · right + row · down
voxel           = floor(point + 0.5)      # on each axis
value           = M[voxel], or 0

The page computes every step the way the program does, in geometry.py and projector.py. The full description is in the algorithm document, and the worked example is its section 5.3.
